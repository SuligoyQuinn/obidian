# 1
Let $p$ be the statement "It is cloudy today"
Let $q$ be the statement "It was cloudy yesterday"
let $r$ be the statement "It is rainy today"
$1$: $p\land q$ Premise
$2$: $q$ Simplification ($2$)
$3$: $q\to r$ Premise
$4$: $r$ Modus ponens ($2$,$3$) 
$5$:$p$ Simplification (2) 
---

$6$: $\therefore p\land r$ Conjunction (5,4)
