# Conditional Probability and Independence
## Joint and Marginal Probabilities
- the joint probability of two events A and B is the probability that they will both occur simultaneously and is the probability of the intersection of the two events $P(A\cap B)$
- the marginal probability of an event is the probability of that event occurring by itself, regardless of whether or not any other events have occurred
## Conditional Probability
- A conditional probability is the probability of an event occurring, given that another event has already occurred
$$
P(B|A) = \frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
$$
