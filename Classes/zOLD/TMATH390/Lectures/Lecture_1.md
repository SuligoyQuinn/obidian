# Statistics and Samples
## Populations and Parameters
- a populations is a collection or set of all objects or measurements that are of interest to the collector
- a parameter is any quantity of a statistical population that summarizes or describes data
- a sample is a subset of data selected from a population
- a statistic is any quantity computed from values in a sample
population mean $\mu$ and sample mean $\bar{x}$
population proportion $p$ and sample proportion $\hat{p}$
population variance $\sigma^2$ and sample variation $s^2$
population standard deviation $\sigma$ and sample standard deviation $s$

## Sampling
Standard random sample
- a sample selected in a way that every element of the pop has an equal chance of being chosen
Systematic Sample
- a sample where every $K$th in the sampling frame is selected
Stratified Sample
- Take the pop and divide into groups, take samples from groups
Cluster Sample
- Population forms natural groups, randomly sample clusters, anyone in cluster is in sample
Multistage Sample
- collect data from whole sample, followed by additional data collection either at the same time or later
