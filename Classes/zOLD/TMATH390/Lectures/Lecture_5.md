# Random Experiments
- activity or situation for which the outcome depends on chance. Repeating the activity may result in different outcomes
# Outcomes, Sample Space, Events
- an outcome is one of the possible results in a random experiment
- the sample space S for a random experiment is the set of all possible outcomes
- A subspace of S consisting of one ore more outcomes is an Event
# Classical Probability
- If all events  in S are equally likely then the classical probability of event E is defined as
$$
P(E)=\frac{|E|}{|S|}
$$
# Frequentist Probability
- if all outcomes not all equally likely, use frequentist probability
- the frequentist probability of event E defined as the limit of its relative frequency in infinitely many trials
- let n be the number of trials of the experiment that are preformed and let k be the number of times that the experiment results in event E occurring. Then the frequentist probability is
$$
P(E)=\lim_{ n \to \infty } \frac{k}{n}
$$
