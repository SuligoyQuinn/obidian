# CLT for Discrete Random Variables
## Bernoulli
$$
\bar{X} \sim N\left( p,\sqrt{ \frac{p(1-p)}{n} } \right)
$$
### Exact
$$
P(\bar{X}\leq \bar{x})=F_{x}(n*\bar{x};n,p)
$$
## Binomial
$$
\bar{X}\sim N\left( np,\sqrt{\frac{np(1-p)}{n} } \right)
$$
### Exact
$$
P(\bar{X}\leq \bar{x})=F_{x}(n*\bar{x};n*m,p)
$$
## Poisson
$$
\bar{X}\sim N\left( \lambda,\sqrt{ \frac{\lambda}{n} } \right)
$$
$$
P(\bar{X}\leq \bar{x})=F_{x}(n*\bar{x};n*\lambda)
$$
## Geometric
$$
\bar{X}\sim N\left( \frac{1-p}{p},\sqrt{ \frac{1-p}{np^2} } \right)
$$
