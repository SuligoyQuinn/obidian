# Data Visualization
## Data Distribution
- _distribution_ of data refers to which values a variable can take on and how often it does
## Frequency Table
- shows the range of values of a variable and how many observations fall within those ranges
## Visual Descriptions
### Skew
- Measure of the asymmetry of a distribution
- More values to left = Right (positive) skew
	- mean is larger than median
- More values to right = Left (negative) skew
	- mean is smaller than median
### Modality
- Unimodal
	- One  major peak
- Bimodal
	- Two major peak
- Multimodal
	- Many peaks
	- when data is multimodal, single measure of centrality and variability will be misleading
