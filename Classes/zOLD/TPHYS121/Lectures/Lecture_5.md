## **Vector Components & Coordinate Systems**
- Already dealt with 1-dimensional motion
- In 2-dimensional motion, motion vector will be angled
$$
\vec{A}=(A,\theta)
$$
- $A$ is magnitude
- $\theta$ is direction
### Vector Decomposition
- Project $\vec{A}$ onto the x and y axis
- These are the x and y components of the vectors
$$\vec{A}_{x}+\vec{A}_{y}=\vec{A}$$
Magnitude of Vector A
$$
|\vec{A}|=\sqrt{ (A_{x})^2+(A_{y})^2 }
$$
Angle
$$
\theta=\arctan |\frac{A_{y}}{A_{x}}|
$$
## Inclined Plane (acceleration)
- Block is moving down a frictionless inclined plane and speed up. Acceleration is down the ramp and is a component of the free fall acceleration 
- Angle coordinate system so positive x aligns with the ramp
- $\cos \theta=\frac{a_{y}}{g}$ $\sin \theta=\frac{a_{x}}{g}$
- $\vec{a}=g\sin \theta$, parallel to the incline
- For $\theta$ between 0 and 90 degrees
### Unit Vector
- Used to describe the direction of the vector components
- called unit vector because vector has a magnitude of one
- Results in not changing the magnitude of the vector components
- $\hat{i}$ represents the direction of the x-axis
- $\hat{j}$ represents the direction of the y-axis
- $\hat{k}$ represents the direction of the z-axis
#### Vector Components using Unit vectors
$$
\vec{A}=\vec{A}_{x}+\vec{A}_{y}
$$
$$
\vec{A}=A_{x}\hat{i}+A_{y}\hat{j}
$$
### Vector Algebra
- Calculations are done by component
$$
\vec{C}=\vec{A}+\vec{B}

$$
$$
= (A_{x}+B_{x})\hat{i}+(A_{y}+B_{y})\hat{j}
$$
