## Energy Conservation Review
$$
E=U_{g}+K=mgy+\frac{1}{2}mv^2
$$
$$
E=U_{sp}+K=\frac{1}{2}k(\nabla x)^2+\frac{1}{2}mv^2
$$
## Work and Potential Energy ( $W_{g}$ and $\nabla U_{g}$)
If a block is dropped from a height of h
- work done by the gravitational force is positive
- the gravitational potential energy is decreasing
$$
W_{g}=-\nabla U_{g}
$$
### $W_{sp}$ and $\nabla U_{sp}$
- the work done by the spring force is negative
- the elastic potential energy is increasing
$$
W_{sp}=-\nabla U_{sp}
$$
## Conservative Force and Potential Energy
- A conservative force has a potential energy function associated
- Work done by conservative forces decreases the potential energy of the system
- allows conversion between kinetic and potential energy
- work done by a conservative force doesn't depend on the path taken
## Non-conservative Forces
- work done by non-conservative forces depends on the path taken, and is not zero if you return to where you started
## Energy Principle
$$
W_{net}=\nabla E
$$
- W is the work done by all the forces acting on the object
$$
W_{net}=W_{c}+W_{nc}
$$
- $W_{c}$  work done by conservative forces
- $W_{nc}$ work done by nonconservative forces
If work is not done by only conservative forces
$$
U_{i}+K_{i}+W_{ext}=U_{f}+K_{f}
$$
