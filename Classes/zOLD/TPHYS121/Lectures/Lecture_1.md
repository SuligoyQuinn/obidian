### Conversions
- Example: width of a book in meters to inches
$width=0.216m$
$1in=2.54*10^{-2}m$
$$
0.216m=0.216m\frac{1in}{2.54*10^{-2}m}
$$
$$
0.216m=\frac{0.216in}{2.54*10^{-2}}
$$
$$
0.216m=8.5in
$$
- Example: height of a door in inches to centimeters
$$
height=75in
$$
$$
75in=\frac{2.54m}{1in}
$$
$$
0.216in=190.5cm
$$


- Adding or subtracting units must match
## **Significant Figures**
- Trailing Zeroes are not significant
	- 760 has 2 sigfigs
- Leading zeroes after decimal are not significant
	- 0.0314 has 3 sigfigs
- Trailing zeroes after decimal are significant
	- 34.00 has 4 sigfigs
- sigfigs in answers are set by fewest
## **Types of Motion**
- Linear Motion
	- Straight Line
	- Horizontal or Vertical
- Projectile Motion
	- 2D motion
	- Horizontal and Vertical
- Rotational Motion
	- Around a fixed point
	- Like a spinning top
- Circular
	- Type of rotational motion
	- Like a wheel
