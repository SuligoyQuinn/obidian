## Spring Force (Elastic Force, $\vec{F}_{sp}$)
- spring can either be stretched or compressed so it can push and pull
- when its doing neither it is in equilibrium
Hooke's Law
$$
\vec{F}_{sp}=-k\nabla \vec{s}
$$
$\nabla \vec{s}$ : stretched/compressed length
$k$ (N/m): the spring constant of the spring 

## Massless String Approximation
- Mass of a string between two objects is much less than the objects
- Treat string as massless for for transmitting forces without changing magnitude
$$
T_{a On String}=T_{bOnString}

$$
