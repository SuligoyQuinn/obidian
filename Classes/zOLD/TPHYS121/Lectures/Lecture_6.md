## X and Y motion are independent
- Two balls dropped at the same time with red one fired to the right
	- Both have vertical initial velocity at 0
- Under influence of gravity only, we can analyze projectile motion as
	- Horizontal motion with constant velocity
	- Vertical motion with constant acceleration
	- $a_{x}=0$ $|a_{y}|=g$
### Projectile motion
- Projectile is an object that moves in 2-dimensions under influence of gravity only
- Decompose initial velocity into x and y components
## Range Equation
$$
x_{f}=\frac{(v_{i})^2\sin(2\theta)}{g}
$$
- Derived from fundamental equations
- Works when projectile lands at the same height it is launched form
