## What is the rotational analog to mass
- Inertia $m$ : an object's resistance to change its linear motion
- Moment of Inertia $I$ : an objects resistance to change its rotational motion
- axis of rotation matters
- greater when center of mass is further away from axis of rotation
for point masses
$$
I=\sum(r_{i})^2m_{i}
$$
for extended body
$$
I=\int r^2
dm
$$
## Rotational Kinetic Energy $K_{rot}$
$$
K_{rot}=\frac{1}{2}I\omega^2
$$
$$
U=U_{g}+U_{sp}
$$
$$
K=K_{trans}+K_{rot}
$$
## Rotational Momentum $L$
$$
L=I\omega
$$
