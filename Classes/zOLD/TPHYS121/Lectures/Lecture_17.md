## Power (P)
- power is the rate of transfer of energy
- Work is a source of energy transfer
Average Power
$$
P_{avg}=\frac{\nabla E}{\nabla t}=\frac{W}{\nabla t}
$$
Instantaneous Power
$$
P(t)=\frac{dE}{dt}
$$
SI unit: J/s = W(Watt)
$$
P=\frac{W}{\nabla t}=\frac{\vec{F}\cdot \nabla \vec{s}}{\nabla t}=\vec{F}\cdot\left( \frac{\nabla \vec{s}}{\nabla t} \right)=\vec{F}\cdot \vec{v}
$$
## Potential Energy
- results from position of configuration
- associated with reversible changes in the configuration state of a system of two interacting objects
- Can be converted entirely to kinetic energy
### Elastic (spring) potential energy
Unstretched spring
$$
U_{sp}=0
$$
Stretched spring
$$
U_{sp}=\frac{1}{2}k(\nabla x)^2
$$
### Gravitational potential energy
$$
U_{g}=mgy
$$
### Conservation of Mechanical Energy
- total mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic energy and potential energy
$$
E=K+U
$$
- in an isolated system the total energy of the system is unchanged
$$
\nabla E_{sys}=0
$$
- if we chose the system to include Earth, and/or Spring
$$
U_{sys,i}+K_{sys,i}=U_{sys,f}+K_{sys,f}
$$
