## **Constant Acceleration**
### Motion
- Acceleration changes velocity: speeding up, slowing down, or changing direction
Position: $\vec{x}$
Velocity: $\vec{v}=\frac{d\vec{x}}{dt}$
Acceleration: $\vec{a}=\frac{d\vec{v}}{dt}$
Displacement: $\nabla \vec{x}=\int_{t_{i}}^{t_{f}}\vec{v}(t)$
Change of Velocity: $\nabla \vec{v}=\int_{t_{i}}^{t_{f}}\vec{a}(t)$
### Equations

## **Free Fall**
- the motion of an object under the influence of gravity only
- ignore air resistance
- Galileo hypothesized that
	- free fall occurs the same way for all objects regardless of mass and shape
	- speed increases linearly with time of flight
		- Acceleration of free falling objects is constant
magnitude of the acceleration $\vec{g}$ or $\vec{a}_{g}$ due to gravity near the earth surface is $|\vec{g}|=+9.8m/s^2$
