## Dynamics of Circular Motion
- Because of centripetal acceleration there must be a net force pointing towards the center of the rotation
- tension, friction, gravity, and normal force may act as a centripetal force
$$
\sum F=ma_{c}
$$
$$
a_{c}=\frac{v^2}{r}
$$
$$
a_{c}=r\omega ^2
$$
### r-t-z Coordinate System
Newton's 2nd law applied in three different axis:
- Radial Direction (r)
- Tangential direction (t)
- Vertical direction (z)
$$
\sum F_{r}=ma_{r}=m\frac{v^2}{r}
$$
$$
\sum F_{t}=ma_{t}
$$
$$
\sum F_{z}=ma_{z}
$$
