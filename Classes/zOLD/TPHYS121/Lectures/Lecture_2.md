## **Motion Diagram**
- Used to visualize motion
- Composite photos at equal time intervals
- 4 types of linear motion
	- at rest
	- constant
	- speeding up
	- slowing down
- Use numbered dots to represent motion
## **Scaler vs Vector**
- Scaler
	- Magnitude Only
	- Positive, Negative, or zero
- Vector
	- Magnitude and Direction
	- Positive or zero
## **Describing Motion**
- Physical quantities are vectors
- Position
	- s or x or y
	- Where is the object relative to the origin
- Velocity
	- v
	- How quickly and in which direction is the object move
- Acceleration
	- a
	- Does the object change its velocity
## **Position Vector vs Displacement**
### Position Vector
- Vector drawn from origin to objects position
### Displacement Vector
- Change of position
- Direct path from initial to final
$$
\nabla \vec{x}=\vec{x}_{f}-\vec{x}_{i}
$$
## **Average Speed and Average Velocity**
### Average Speed
Scaler
$$
v_{Avg}=\frac{d}{\nabla t}
$$
distance traveled over time interval spent traveling
### Average Velocity
Vector
$$
\vec{v}_{Avg}=\frac{\nabla \vec{s}}{\nabla t}=\frac{\vec{s}_{f}-\vec{s}_{i}}{\nabla t}
$$
displacement over time interval spent traveling
gives us the rate of change of position
$$
\vec{s}_{f}=\vec{s}_{i}+\vec{v}\nabla t
$$
