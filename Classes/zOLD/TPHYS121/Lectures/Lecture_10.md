## Friction
### Kinetic Friction $\vec{f}_{k}$
- a type of contact force caused by the interaction between rough surfaces
- kinetic friction opposes motion between two surfaces, slows down the sliding motion
- always in the opposite direction to the direction of motion, tangent to the surface
- depends on the normal force and the coefficient of kinetic friction
$$
f_{k}=\mu _{k}F_{n}
$$
- $\mu$ depends on the type of surface
### Static friction $\vec{f}_{s}$
- prevents relative motion between two surfaces, keeps object stuck
- static friction is opposite to the direction that the object would move on a frictionless surface
#### Maximum Static friction $\vec{f}_{s,max}$
$$
f_{s,max}=\mu_{s}F_{n}
$$
- The maximum force needed to push an object before it moves
