# Macromolecules
## Major Classes
- Carbohydrates
- Proteins
- Nucleic acids
- Lipids
## Polymers
- Most biological macromolecules are polymers
	- chains of similar molecules linked by covalent bonds
	- monomers are the molecules
- Formed by dehydration reactions
	- molecule of water is removed and that forms polymer
	- Hydrolysis is the opposite, water added to polymer to break it
## Chemical Structures
- Carbon and Hydrogen not listed in structures
## Carbohydrates
- Monomer of Carb is _Monosaccharide_
	- one sugar
- Polymer of Carb is _Polysaccharide_
	- many sugar
### Disaccharides
- sucrose (glucose + fructose)
- lactose
- -ose refers to sugar
- used to store energy
	- Starch in plants
	- Glycogen in animals
## Proteins
- make up 50% of the dry mass of cells
- composed of amino acids
	- monomers
	- unique R groups
- polypeptides
	- polymers
- order of amino acids control shape and function of protein
## Nucleic acids
- Nucleotide
	- Monomer
- Polynucleotide Strand
	- Polymer
	- DNA or RNA
- Stores and transmits genetic information
	- using ATGC
- Three components
	- Phosphate
		- Negatively charged
	- Sugar
	- Nitrogenous Base
- DNA is dual stranded
- RNA is single stranded
## Lipids
- Mostly carbon and hydrogen
- nonpolar
- Fats and Steroids
- Phospholipids main component of cell membranes
