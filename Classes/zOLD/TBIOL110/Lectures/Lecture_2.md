# Atoms
- Subatomic Particles
	- Protons +charge
	- Neutrons neutral charge
	- Electrons -charge ($e^-$)
- Protons and Neutrons in the Nucleus
- Electrons orbit nucleus
## Elements
- Elements are specific types of atoms
	- Based on number of protons
- Elements arranged by mass in periodic table

## Drawing Atoms
- First shell can hold 2 electrons
- Every other can hold 8 electrons
- Fill shell then move to next
## Ions
- Element with a charge
- ex) $Na^+$ $Ca^2+$
## Electrons and Chemical Reactions
- Valence shell is outer electron shell
- Atoms want full valence shell
- Chemical reactions when the move electrons
- Strong bonds when not full
# pH
- Potential of Hydrogen
- Measure of Hydrogen ions
- Logarithmic scale
- Further from 7 more basic or acidic
## Acids
- add hydrogen ions
## Bases
- removes hydrogen ions
# Chemical Bonds
## Ionic Bond
- Electrons lost from one atom and gained by another atom
- Ions extremely important in biology
- Ionic bonds really rare in organisms though
- water dissolved ionic bonds
## Covalent Bonds
- Electrons are shared
- Strongest bond
- Single Bonds (H-H)
	- 2 electrons involved
- Double bond (O=O)
	- 4 electrons involved
### Nonpolar
- equal pull on electrons
- electrons spend equal time around both atoms
### Polar
- unequal pull on electrons
- partial charges
- look for O, N, F
	- Except CO2
## Hydrogen Bonds
- Partial positive Hydrogen bonded with a partial negative region on a neighboring molecule
# Polarity
- Molecules with mostly polar bonds = polar molecules
	- Hydrophilic
- Molecules with mostly non-polar bonds = nonpolar molecules
	- Hydrophobic
- polar polar mix
- polar ions mix
- nonpolar nonpolar mix
