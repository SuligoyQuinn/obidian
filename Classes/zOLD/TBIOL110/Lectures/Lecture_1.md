## Levels at which life is studied?
- Ecosystem
	- Community + non-living parts of habitat
- Community
	- Multiple populations that interact in an area
- Population
	- Group of same species
	- Close enough to mate
- Organism
- Cellular
- Atomic
## 3 Domains of Life
Any living thing of earth fits into 3 domains
- Prokaryotes have no nucleus just DNA
	- Bacteria
	- Archaea
- Eukaryotes have a nucleus
	- Eukarya
