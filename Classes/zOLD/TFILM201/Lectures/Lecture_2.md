# Narrative Form
- What is *Narrative*
	- Story; string of events occurring in space and time
	- Ordered by logic of cause and effect
	- Focus on human characters and their struggles
		- pursue goals and encounter obstacles along the way
- *Narrative Expectations* in Classical Hollywood
	- Clarity
		- setting
		- time
		- events
	- Unity
		- cause and effect
	- Characters
		- identification
		- heroic
		- likeable
	- Closure
		- Hollywood Ending
## Framing the Fictional World
- Story vs Plot
	- *Story*
		- complete chronological story
	- *Plot*
		- abbreviated, reorganized events as they appear on screen
## Perspective
- *Perspective*
	- From whose POV is the story told
- Point-of-view(POV) shot & spectator identification
- Other techniques provide access to *character's subjectivity*
	- Voice-overs
	- Direct-address
		- Looking into camera
# Three-act Structure
- Act 1
	- exposition leads to turning point
- Act II
	- complications lead to climax
- Act III
	- falling action leads to resolution
# Restricted vs. Unrestricted Narration
- Restricted
	- 1st person narration
	- tied to a particular character
	- only know what the character(s) know
- Unrestricted
	- 3rd person omniscient narration
	- viewers are not tied to a specific character
	- info characters do not possess
- Films oscillate between the two
